Privacy

This page explains, in plain terms, what data Crystal Studio stores as part of using this site and why. It is project/store documentation, not a formal legal privacy policy, and does not claim compliance with any specific data protection regulation or certification.

Account Data

When you register, we store your name, email, and password (hashed, never in plain text) so you can log in and place orders. A login session token is kept in your browser's local storage until you log out or it expires.

Order Data

Placing an order stores the items, quantities, prices, and status of that order against your account so it can appear in My Orders and its details page.

Addresses

Shipping addresses you save under My Addresses are stored against your account so you can reuse them at checkout instead of retyping them each time.

Wishlist

Products you add to your wishlist are stored against your account and are only visible to you.

Reviews

A review you submit is linked to your account and displayed publicly on the product's page together with your display name and star rating. Store administrators can remove reviews that violate site guidelines.

Browser Storage (Recently Viewed & Cart)

Some features - like your shopping cart and recently viewed products - are kept only in your browser's local storage, not on our servers. This data stays on your device, is not shared between devices or browsers, and is cleared if you clear your browser data.

Questions

If you have questions about the data described here, please reach out through the Contact page.
