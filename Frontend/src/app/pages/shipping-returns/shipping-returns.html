Shipping & Returns

This page describes how orders actually move through {{ siteName }} today, based on the order statuses used across the site.

Order Status

Every order moves through Placed → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered. You can follow this on the order details page for any order from My Orders. There is currently no courier tracking number or third-party tracking link attached to an order - the status shown on the site is the most up-to-date information available.

Shipping Times

Once an order is Shipped, it is typically delivered within 3–7 business days depending on your location and regional courier transit. Your order page displays the current dispatch and delivery status.

Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order yourself from its order details page while it is still Pending or Confirmed. Once an order has moved to Shipped, self-service cancellation is no longer available - please contact us if you need help with an order that has already shipped.

Returns & Refunds

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery - for example if an item arrived damaged, you received the wrong item, or it isn't as described. Open the order under My Orders and choose Request a Return.

We review every request and email you at each step. Once your return is approved, send the items back with your order number included. When they arrive we issue your refund: card payments are refunded to the same card (it can take 5-7 business days to appear), and Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by our team directly.

Orders that haven't shipped yet can simply be cancelled from the order page instead - card payments for cancelled orders are refunded automatically. For anything else, please get in touch with your order number.

Out-of-Stock Items

Product availability is shown on every product page. Items that are out of stock cannot be added to a cart or purchased until restocked, so an order will only ever contain items that were in stock at the time of purchase.
